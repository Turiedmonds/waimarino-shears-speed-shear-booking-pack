Step 1

Hire Information

This page explains what is included, what the organiser must provide and what is required for setup.

What we provide

  • Two-stand speed shear stand
  • Two Heiniger Evo shearing plants
  • Speed Shear Timing System
  • Two TVs for displaying timing, points and draws
  • Devices required for judging interaction with the timing system
  • Catching pens incorporated into the speed shear stand
  • Backup manual timing equipment, including stopwatches
  • Extension leads and cable reels required for our supplied equipment
  • Transport, setup, testing and pack-down of our equipment
  • A minimum of three Waimarino Shearing personnel to operate the system
  • Competition roster forms, if required, to assist with competitor entries

What the organiser provides

  • Stage, trailer or suitable ground-level setup area
  • Suitable electrical power supply
  • Lighting
  • Sound / PA system
  • Generator, if required
  • Any sheep yards or pens required beyond our catching pens
  • Competition judges
  • Sheep-handling personnel
  • Staff for competitor entries, entry fees and sign-in

Our staff operate the timing system. They do not undertake judging, competitor payment/entry handling or sheep-handling roles.

Space required

4.8 m wide × 2.9 m deep × 2.45 m high

The shearing board itself is 1.8 m from front to back. The 2.9 m overall depth is measured from the front edge of the board to the rear catching-pen fence.

Additional room is required around the stand for safe access and the timing-system operating area.

Setup and venue requirements

Allow approximately four hours for setup and testing, provided the required stage/setup area, power and other organiser-supplied infrastructure are ready when our team arrives.

For early-start competitions, setup may need to occur the day before. Equipment left onsite overnight must be kept secure and protected from weather.

Indoor setup is preferred. Waimarino Shearing Incorporated may delay, stop or decline setup or operation where conditions present an unreasonable safety risk to people or equipment.

Read Hire Terms & Conditions

Hire fee and payment

The standard hire fee is NZ$750 plus GST. Travel to and from the event is included. A NZ$300 deposit is required no later than 14 days before the event. The remaining balance is payable within 14 days following completion of the event. Separate invoices will be issued for the deposit and remaining balance.

If the deposit has not been received by the due date following reasonable reminders, Waimarino Shearing Incorporated may treat the booking as cancelled.

Accommodation

Accommodation is not included in the standard hire fee. Where accommodation is reasonably required because of travel, event timing or a day-before setup, Waimarino Shearing Incorporated will arrange the accommodation and charge the organiser the actual accommodation cost in addition to the hire fee. Where reasonably possible, the organiser will be advised of the cost before the accommodation is booked.

Cancellation

If the organiser cancels before the Waimarino Shearing team has departed for the event, amounts already paid will be refunded except for any non-refundable accommodation or other costs already incurred specifically for that booking.

If cancellation occurs after our team has departed for the event, the NZ$300 deposit is non-refundable. Any non-refundable accommodation costs already incurred also remain payable.

If Waimarino Shearing Incorporated is unable to fulfil the booking, amounts paid by the organiser will be refunded.

Postponement

Where an event is postponed before our team has departed, the booking and deposit may be transferred to one new mutually agreed date, subject to Waimarino Shearing Incorporated being available. If we are unable to service the proposed replacement date, amounts paid will be refunded except for any non-refundable costs already incurred.

If an event is postponed after our team has departed, the original NZ$300 deposit is retained. A replacement event date will be treated as a new booking and will require a new deposit.

Equipment and operating conditions

The organiser must take reasonable care to protect equipment supplied by Waimarino Shearing Incorporated. The organiser may be responsible for reasonable repair or replacement costs where equipment is damaged through negligence, misuse or deliberate actions by the organiser, its personnel, competitors, or others for whom the organiser is responsible.

Waimarino Shearing Incorporated may delay, stop or decline setup or operation where conditions present an unreasonable safety risk to people or equipment.

Competition operation

The organiser remains responsible for the running and administration of the competition, including judging, sheep handling, competitor entries, entry fees and sign-in. Waimarino Shearing Incorporated is responsible for setting up and operating its timing system and supplied equipment using the competition information provided by the organiser.
